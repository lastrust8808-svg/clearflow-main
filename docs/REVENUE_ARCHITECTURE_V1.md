# ClearFlow Revenue Architecture v1

ClearFlow can generate revenue from the same operating spine it already controls:

## Primary Revenue Layers

1. Embedded business accounts
2. Connected cards and card programs
3. Bill pay and payment execution
4. Processor settlement and payout analytics
5. Premium AI reporting, trust, tax, and treasury packets
6. Yield and reserve sweep posture
7. Capital and credit readiness

## What ClearFlow Already Has

- connected bank and financial account profiles
- chart-of-accounts automation for connected accounts and wallets
- payment, remittance, reconciliation, and settlement control
- treasury, reserve, collateral, borrowing, and liquidation records
- rewards and membership program posture

## Best Monetization Order

1. Business accounts, cards, and bill pay
2. Processor settlement and merchant cashflow analytics
3. Premium reporting and AI packet workflows
4. Yield, sweep, and reserve optimization
5. Capital or credit offer posture

## Product Principle

Revenue should come from:

- helping users operate their entities
- reducing workflow fragmentation
- executing or proving real financial activity

Revenue should not come from:

- confusing internal credits with money
- promising yield without a real partner rail
- pretending staged rails are live

## UI Surfaces

- Overview: Revenue & Yield
- Settings: Revenue Architecture
- AI Studio: Revenue & Embedded Finance Strategy
