## Upload Title
How To Add And Save A Bill In ClearFlow

## YouTube Description
This ClearFlow Academy walkthrough shows how to add a bill, review extracted bill details, save the bill to the active entity, find it again in Accounting, and start the payment workflow with Pay This Bill.

ClearFlow is designed so users can confirm that saved bills appear in Accounting records, connect to supporting documents, and move into payment review instead of disappearing after entry.

In this video:
- Open Accounting from the left panel
- Use Add Bill
- Upload or manually enter bill details
- Save the bill to the active entity
- Verify the bill appears in the Bills section
- Review payment status
- Use Pay This Bill when ready to start the payment workflow

Important: payment execution depends on the connected provider, bank rail, biller route, and payment readiness status. A saved bill is an accounting record; payment release should be verified through the payment status, settlement trail, bank/provider confirmation, and supporting documents.

## Narration Script
Welcome back to ClearFlow Academy. In this walkthrough, we are focusing on one practical workflow: adding a bill, saving it, finding it again, and starting the payment process.

Start from the left panel and open Accounting. Accounting is where bills, invoices, remittances, bank feed, chart of accounts, journals, payments, and reconciliation live.

Use Add Bill to enter the bill. If you have a file, upload the bill image or PDF. If you do not have a file, use manual entry.

After extraction or manual entry, review the important fields: vendor name, amount due, due date, bill number or account reference, and any supporting document.

When you press Save Bill, ClearFlow should retain the bill under the active entity. The validation step is important: return to the Bills section and confirm the bill appears there.

Once the bill is visible, review the payment state. If no payment has been started yet, it should show as not submitted. When the bill is ready, use Pay This Bill to open the payment workflow.

Payment execution is separate from saving the bill. A saved bill creates the accounting record. Payment release depends on the connected bank, provider readiness, payment rail, approval posture, and settlement confirmation.

The key habit is: save, find, then verify. If the bill was saved correctly, it should appear in Bills, connect to any supporting document, show payment status, and allow a payment action when the workflow is ready.
