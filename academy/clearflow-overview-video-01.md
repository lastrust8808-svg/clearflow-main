## Narration Script
Welcome to ClearFlow Academy. In this first walkthrough, we are going to take a quick tour of the ClearFlow workspace and show how the major sections connect.

ClearFlow is built around one simple idea: the left panel is where you choose the major desk, and the main workspace is where you complete the work inside that desk. If you want to move from Accounting to Documents, Entities, Investments, or Settings, you use the left panel. Once you are inside a desk, use the buttons and tabs inside that desk to stay in that workflow.

Start with the Overview. This is the command center. It gives a high-level view of what needs attention, what has been created, and where the next action should happen.

Next, open Entities. Entities are the foundation of the workspace. A trust, business, or individual profile should be created here first so records, accounting, documents, and authority proof have the right home. If authority proof is missing or names do not match, ClearFlow can keep collecting data, but transaction release should remain paused until the authority posture is corrected.

Now move into Accounting. Accounting is where bills, invoices, remittances, journal entries, chart of accounts, bank feed, reconciliation, and payment actions live. If a user uploads or enters a bill, the important validation is that it appears again inside the Bills section, can be opened, and has a payment status. When a bill is ready, the Pay This Bill action starts the payment workflow.

Documents and Vault is where uploaded files, generated packets, receipts, bills, evidence, and retained proof are organized. If an accounting item came from an upload, users should be able to find the supporting document in the vault or through the linked accounting record.

Bank Feed and Payments show whether live connections and payment rails are ready. ClearFlow distinguishes connected bank data, ACH readiness, wire readiness, check generation, positive pay, biller-direct limitations, and provider profiles. A rail should only be treated as ready when the configured provider or bank channel actually supports it.

Investments is where users can model real estate deals, prepare 1031 exchange planning checklists, compare funding paths, and save strategy plans of action. The goal here is education, planning, and retained decision support before any live-money execution.

The most important habit in ClearFlow is verification. After every save or submit action, confirm the new record appears in the right desk. For example, a saved bill should appear in Bills, a connected provider should appear in Bank Feed and the chart of accounts, a saved investment plan should appear under Saved Investment Plans, and uploaded evidence should remain available in Documents and Vault.

That is the basic ClearFlow navigation pattern: choose the desk from the left panel, complete the action inside the desk, and verify the result in the matching records, ledger, vault, or status view.
